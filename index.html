The Problem(s)

Players

Exploration is broken - The App Store is not designed for effortlessly exploring its catalog of over a million games. Players must download a game before they can experience whether it is actually fun. Game ads often fail to reflect the real gameplay, making discovery frustrating.

Game Developers

Distribution is broken - User acquisition has become so expensive that only the largest studios can consistently win players' attention. As a result, most indie games struggle to reach an audience.

Content Creators

Content creation is broken - Millions of players want to create and share gaming content, but creating mobile gameplay videos is still difficult. Recording gameplay, capturing reactions, and editing creates a high barrier for aspiring creators.
